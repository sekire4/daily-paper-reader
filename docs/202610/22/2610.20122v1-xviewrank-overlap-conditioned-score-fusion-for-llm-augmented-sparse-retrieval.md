## 摘要
大语言模型可以通过生成查询的替代视图来减少稀疏检索中的词汇不匹配。然而，一旦这些视图被独立检索，它们的排序列表可能互补、冗余或受查询漂移影响，从而使固定融合并非最优。我们提出 XViewRank，一种轻量级的检索侧方法，用于对来自原始查询、假设视图和 step-back 视图的 BM25 结果进行条件融合。对于每个池化候选，XViewRank 保留三个归一化 BM25 分数以及检索结果集之间的三个查询级重叠度。一个小型非线性评分器将重叠度作为上下文，以改变视图特定分数的解释方式，而不联合编码查询和段落文本。在匹配输入协议下，XViewRank 在 TREC DL 2019 和 2020 上均使 nDCG@10 和 MAP 在数值上优于倒数排名融合，并在 DL19 上取得统计显著的 MAP 提升。额外分析表明，当视图排序之间的一致性较低时，收益最明显，而在 DL20 上的证据较弱。结果支持将重叠条件化分数融合作为固定融合的一种紧凑检索侧替代方案，同时也界定了其优势在经验上得到确立的范围。

## Abstract
Large language models can reduce vocabulary mismatch in sparse retrieval by generating alternative views of a query. Once these views are searched independently, however, their ranked lists may be complementary, redundant, or affected by query drift, making fixed fusion suboptimal. We present XViewRank, a lightweight retrieval-side method for conditionally fusing BM25 results from an original query, a hypothesis view, and a step-back view. For each pooled candidate, XViewRank retains three normalized BM25 scores and three query-level overlaps between the retrieved result sets. A small nonlinear scorer uses the overlaps as context that changes how the view-specific scores are interpreted, without jointly encoding query and passage text. Under a matched-input protocol, XViewRank improves nDCG@10 and MAP numerically over reciprocal rank fusion on both TREC DL 2019 and 2020, with a statistically significant MAP gain on DL19. Additional analyses show that the clearest benefit occurs when the view rankings have low agreement, while the evidence on DL20 is weaker. The results support overlap-conditioned score fusion as a compact retrieval-side alternative to fixed fusion, while also delimiting where its advantage is empirically established.
