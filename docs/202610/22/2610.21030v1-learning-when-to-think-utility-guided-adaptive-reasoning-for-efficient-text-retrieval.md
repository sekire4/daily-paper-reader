## 摘要
以推理为条件的嵌入可通过逐步细化语义约束来改进检索。然而，额外的推理可能引入过度泛化或语义噪声较大的抽象，从而削弱检索的特异性。现有方法主要根据最终检索结果来监督推理，对细化过程本身提供的指导有限。我们提出 AURA，一个直接根据中间推理状态区分相关文档与困难干扰项的能力来监督这些状态的框架。AURA 进一步在前缀条件嵌入上训练一个轻量级效用估计器，以在推理时自适应调整推理深度。在推理密集的 BRIGHT 基准上，AURA-4B 在每查询使用 2.8 个推理步骤和 96 个生成 token 的情况下，达到 34.7 的平均 nDCG@10。在匹配的部署条件下，相对于完整轨迹推理，自适应停止将延迟从 4.6 秒降至 0.76 秒。MS MARCO 上的结果进一步表明，这种专门化不必显著降低传统的域内检索性能。

## Abstract
Reasoning-conditioned embeddings can improve retrieval by progressively refining semantic constraints. However, additional reasoning may introduce over-generalized or semantically noisy abstractions that weaken retrieval specificity. Existing methods largely supervise reasoning through final retrieval outcomes, providing limited guidance over the refinement process itself. We propose AURA, a framework that directly supervises intermediate reasoning states according to how well they separate relevant documents from hard distractors. AURA further trains a lightweight utility estimator over prefix-conditioned embeddings to adapt reasoning depth at inference time. On the reasoning-intensive BRIGHT benchmark, AURA-4B achieves 34.7 average nDCG@10 while using 2.8 reasoning steps and 96 generated tokens per query. Under matched deployment conditions, adaptive stopping reduces latency from 4.6 to 0.76 seconds relative to full-trajectory reasoning. Results on MS MARCO further suggest that this specialization need not substantially degrade conventional in-domain retrieval.
