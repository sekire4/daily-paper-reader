## 摘要
ColBERT 等多向量检索模型通常通过在词元级别匹配查询和文档，优于规模相当的单向量模型。然而，为每个词元保留一个向量会显著增加存储需求，并且相比单向量双编码器会减慢检索速度，从而限制其可扩展性。我们提出 FLINT（Fixed-budget Late INTeraction），这是一种使用基于注意力的池化，以固定数量的嵌入向量表示每个查询和文档的架构。与仅对文档表示进行池化而保留词元级查询的架构不同，FLINT 对查询和文档两侧对称地应用池化，从而限制文档存储以及检索期间所需的向量比较次数。FLINT 在 BEIR 上取得了与最接近基线相当的排序效果，并在若干具有更长、更复杂查询的基准上优于它们，包括 BrowseComp+、CRUMB 和 TREC Tip-of-the-Tongue 2025。我们通过在两个独立骨干模型（BERT 和 ModernBERT）上进行测试，证明 FLINT 的有效性-效率优势可扩展到不同的编码器架构。在长查询语料库上，FLINT 的端到端检索速度比最接近的基线 ConstBERT 快 1.5-2.8 倍。相对于 ColBERTv2 等词元级延迟交互模型，FLINT 还将索引大小减小了 5.3-9.3 倍。

## Abstract
Multi-vector retrieval models such as ColBERT often outperform single-vector models of comparable scale by matching queries and documents at the token level. However, retaining a vector for every token substantially increases storage requirements and slows retrieval compared to single-vector bi-encoders, limiting their scalability. We introduce FLINT (Fixed-budget Late INTeraction), an architecture that uses attention-based pooling to represent each query and document with a fixed number of embedding vectors. Unlike architectures that pool only document representations while retaining token-level queries, FLINT applies pooling symmetrically to both sides, limiting document storage and the number of vector comparisons needed during retrieval. FLINT achieves comparable ranking effectiveness to the closest baselines on BEIR and outperforms them on several benchmarks with longer and more complex queries, including BrowseComp+, CRUMB, and TREC Tip-of-the-Tongue 2025. We demonstrate that FLINT's effectiveness-efficiency benefits extend across encoder architectures, testing it on two separate backbones (BERT and ModernBERT). On long-query corpora, FLINT delivers 1.5-2.8 times faster end-to-end retrieval than ConstBERT, the closest baseline. FLINT also reduces index size by 5.3-9.3 times relative to token-level late-interaction models such as ColBERTv2.
