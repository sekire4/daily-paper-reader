## 摘要
检索增强生成（RAG）正越来越多地部署在地理分布的云和边缘基础设施上，在这些环境中，广域延迟、跨区域出口成本和数据驻留规则与检索质量相互制约。传统流水线仅按语义相似度排序，忽略内容所在位置，从而推高尾延迟和成本。我们将地理RAG选择问题（Geo-RAG Selection Problem, GRaSP）形式化，这是一种即插即用的重打分层，它按延迟对相关性进行衰减，并在基数预算和区域上限约束下加入可选的成本惩罚，且无需重新训练。我们首先厘清其复杂度：基础形式是划分拟阵上的线性目标，可由 O(N log N) 贪心算法最优求解，而非背包式建模所暗示的 NP 难；只有在引入可加性出口预算时才会出现难解性。在五节点 CloudLab 测试床上，通过 Natural Questions 语料（268 万段落）模拟四区域广域网，GRaSP 的收益取决于放置方式：在均匀放置下，它以可忽略的质量代价降低抓取延迟；在主题式（类似驻留）放置下，它勾勒出召回率—延迟帕累托前沿，并优于仅相关性、最近 k 和带延迟预算的基线。在中等延迟设置下，GRaSP 将 P90 抓取延迟降低 41%，同时保留仅相关性方法 78% 的召回率，选择开销为亚毫秒级，并在 200 查询/秒下保持稳定。这些发现在四个公开 BEIR 基准上具有泛化性。在端到端问答中，GRaSP 将上下文抓取延迟降低 43%，同时与仅相关性检索的绝对 EM 差距保持在 0.062 以内。

## Abstract
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is increasingly served across geo-distributed cloud and edge infrastructures, where wide-area latency, cross-region egress cost, and data-residency rules compete with retrieval quality. Conventional pipelines rank by semantic similarity alone and ignore where content lives, inflating tail latency and cost. We formalize the Geo-RAG Selection Problem (GRaSP), a drop-in re-scoring layer that decays relevance by latency and adds an optional cost penalty under a cardinality budget and per-region caps, with no retraining. We first settle its complexity: the base formulation is a linear objective over a partition matroid, solved optimally by an O(N log N) greedy algorithm, rather than NP-hard as a knapsack framing suggests, with hardness entering only under an additive egress budget. On a five-node CloudLab testbed emulating a four-region WAN over the Natural Questions corpus (2.68M passages), GRaSP's benefit is placement-dependent: under uniform placement it cuts fetch latency at negligible quality cost; under topical (residency-like) placement it traces a recall-latency Pareto frontier that dominates relevance-only, nearest-k, and a latency-budgeted baseline. At a moderate latency setting, GRaSP lowers P90 fetch latency by 41% while retaining 78% of relevance-only's recall, with sub-millisecond selection overhead, stable to 200 queries/s. Findings generalize across four public BEIR benchmarks. In end-to-end question answering, GRaSP reduces context-fetch latency by 43% while remaining within 0.062 absolute EM of relevance-only retrieval.
