## 摘要
稠密检索器通常使用从词法检索或稠密检索结果中选出的难负例进行训练。然而，检索上的接近性并不能表明某个候选是否与显式的领域结构相冲突。我们提出 CA-HNM，一个约束感知的难负例挖掘框架，它将基于检索的候选生成与从结构化领域模型导出的确定性不兼容规则相结合。该框架移除已知正例，围绕查询目标构建局部类型化上下文，记录关系、范围、目标和层级不匹配，并利用所得候选对标准双编码器进行微调。结构化信息仅在离线负例构建阶段需要。我们在 MOOCCubeX 和 Course-Skill Atlas 上使用两种编码器、两种训练损失和十个随机种子对 CA-HNM 进行评估。相对于检索排名匹配的对照组，CA-HNM-Pure 在八种数据集—编码器—损失配置中的六种上具有更高的平均 NDCG@10。四个分层 95% bootstrap 区间完全高于零，且大多出现在三元组损失下。在一项单独实验中，CA-HNM-Mixed 在两个数据集上均比匹配混合对照组具有略高的平均 NDCG@10。结构变体表明，可用结构的变化也会改变候选资格和负例池组成。结果表明，约束感知选择的贡献取决于训练目标和负例池的组成。

## Abstract
Dense retrievers are commonly trained with hard negatives selected from lexical or dense retrieval results. Retrieval proximity, however, does not indicate whether a candidate conflicts with explicit domain structure. We introduce CA-HNM, a constraint-aware hard-negative mining framework that combines retrieval-based candidate generation with deterministic incompatibility rules derived from a structured domain model. The framework removes known positives, constructs a local typed context around the query target, records relational, scope, target, and level mismatches, and uses the resulting candidates to fine-tune a standard dual encoder. Structured information is required only during offline negative construction. We evaluate CA-HNM on MOOCCubeX and Course-Skill Atlas with two encoders, two training losses, and ten seeds. Relative to a retrieval-rank-matched control, CA-HNM-Pure has higher mean NDCG@10 in six of eight dataset--encoder--loss configurations. Four hierarchical 95% bootstrap intervals are entirely above zero, mostly under triplet loss. In a separate experiment, CA-HNM-Mixed shows slightly higher mean NDCG@10 than a matched-mixed control on both collections. Structural variants show that changes in available structure can also change candidate eligibility and negative-pool composition. The results indicate that the contribution of constraint-aware selection depends on the training objective and the composition of the negative pool.
